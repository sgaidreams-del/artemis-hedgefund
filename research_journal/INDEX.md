## How entries are added

The AutoResearch experiment runner (`src/autoresearch/experiment/runner.py`) appends a row here whenever an experiment completes, and creates a detailed entry under `research_journal/entries/exp_NNNN_description.md`.

Manual entries (e.g. from the weekly review) go under `research_journal/manual/`.
